Sniffer turns the cell towers around you into something you can see and hear.

<b>Look around</b>
Hold up your phone and cell towers appear as beacons in their real direction, with their distance and network type (2G–5G). The tower your phone is connected to pulses. Tap a beacon for details.

<b>Ambient mode</b>
Put your headphones on, lock the screen and go for a walk. Sniffer plays a generative soundscape (made with Pure Data) driven by the network around you:
<ul>
<li>a drone that thickens as you walk into areas with more and closer towers</li>
<li>crackly radio static that follows your live signal quality</li>
<li>synth phrases when it gets dense, or when your phone switches to another tower</li>
</ul>

<b>Tower data</b>
Tower positions come from OpenCellID, an open database (CC BY-SA 4.0), mirrored monthly by the project. Sniffer downloads your country's towers once (it knows the country from your phone's cell, not your location) and then works offline. No account needed. If you have an OpenCellID API key you can add it for live, fresher data around you; then the map area around you is sent to OpenCellID.
